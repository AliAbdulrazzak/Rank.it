Rank.it Privacy Policy

The Rank.it App stores some information locally on your device, including app settings such as dark mode, color theme, font scale, and local vote-limit data used by the app.

The app also sends and stores shared poll information through Google Firebase Firestore, including poll titles, category names, option names, vote totals, and related timestamps so polls can be displayed to users.

The app does not currently request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library, and it does not require account creation for the current version of the app.

Information that you enter into polls, categories, or options may be visible to other users of the app, so you should not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information through shared poll content.

If we inadvertently end up with any personal information of yours through user-submitted content, our policy is to delete it as soon as it is discovered.
